Is Bitcoin a scam?

No, Bitcoin is not a scam. Bitcoin has a clean and verifiable track record starting from 2009 and this is expected to continue far into the future. It has had thousands of highly skilled and reputable developers working on it for decades and it's fully open-source and independently auditable. If used properly, Bitcoin works exactly as the whitepaper, documentation and code suggests. You can verify everything by yourself by using Bitcoin and following best practices like self custody, running your own node and good digital hygiene. Is Bitcoin secure?

Yes, Bitcoin is completely secure. The cryptography protecting Bitcoin is sound and battle-tested. As long as you keep your private keys safe, there is no significant risk for losing your Bitcoins. Loss of Bitcoins happens mostly because of human errors like loss of private keys, sending Bitcoins to a wrong address, falling for social engineering scams or keeping your Bitcoins on an exchange unnecessarily. However, this doesn't mean Bitcoin is completely immune to security threats like quantum computers, AI or competing forks. Solutions for possible threats like quantum, AI and forks are constantly being worked and it's very probable that solutions and fixes will arrive on time. Bitcoin has had plenty of security threats and bugs in the past and adequate fixes and solutions have always arrived on time, hence Bitcoin's nearly spotless track record and uptime.
